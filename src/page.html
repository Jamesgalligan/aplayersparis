Is this a traditional mastermind held by A Players?

No. The Global Tour weekends run a main stage, a thousand attendees and a gala. Paris is a dedicated private mastermind: fifteen founders, three advisors, and three days of closed-door workshops and one-on-one breakdowns.

What do the one-on-one sessions involve?

Day Two runs on set times. Every founder gets one-on-one slots with all three advisors, Christian on AI, Marius on ads and James on the business behind both, with mini-roundtables in between. Everything is booked in advance, so nobody waits.

Where in Paris is it?

We're confirming the venue now. Everyone holding a seat hears first, well ahead of the weekend.

What's included in the ticket?

All three days, your one-on-one sessions with all three advisors, dinner every evening and the Moulin Rouge experience on the closing night. Flights and accommodation are not included.

Is there a members discount?

Yes. A Players Club members take 25% off whichever price is live, so €4,000 becomes €3,000 while the early seats last. Use your member code at checkout.

Why does the price go up?

The first ten seats are €4,000. Once those are taken the remaining five are €5,000. It is the same room and the same three days either way. The earlier you commit, the less you pay.

Where does AI fit in?

Christian leads one of Day One's three roundtable sections on AI and the systems underneath your business, and it carries into your one-on-one sessions with him on Day Two.

Do I need to attend all three days?

Yes. Day One sets the context, Day Two is where the work gets done, and Day Three closes the weekend. There is no single-day ticket.

Who should attend?

Established entrepreneurs with revenue already coming in, who want three days of direct attention on their business in a room of fifteen.
